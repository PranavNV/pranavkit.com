01 — About

I study what AI agents do once they leave the lab (what they cite, how they talk to each other, who they simulate) and build the evaluations that tell us when to trust them.

I'm a Research Scientist at Salesforce AI Research on the Interactive AI team. Most of my work sits where agentic systems meet trust and safety. In deep research and autoresearch, I audit answer engines and deep research agents for unsupported claims and one-sided sourcing (DeepTRACE, ICLR 2026; Search Engines in the AI Era, FAccT 2025). I also help build enterprise deep research systems that know when they have enough evidence to stop (ACL 2026) and write reports that stay faithful to their sources, and I train reasoning models to propose diverse, grounded scientific ideas (Diverse Scientific Ideas, 2026).

A second thread is AI simulation and persona alignment. LLM personas increasingly stand in for real users, so I ask who those personas actually represent (A Tale of Two Identities, AAAI 2026), how to ground them in social science (InterviewSim, COLM 2026), and how AI companions drift out of character over long conversations. I also study agent-to-agent communication: how persuasion and beliefs cascade through LLM agent networks (Belief Cascades) and how images sway vision-language agents (MMPersuade and Seeing is Believing, EMNLP 2026).

All of it is human-centered AI at heart, and it grows out of my Ph.D. in Informatics at Penn State with Dr. Shomir Wilson, where I studied sociodemographic bias in NLP across disability, nationality, race, and culture. That work earned an Outstanding Paper Award at EMNLP 2023, a Best Short Paper Award at TrustNLP, a DEI Recognition Award at CSCW 2024, and an Honorable Mention for Penn State's Best AI Dissertation. I serve as an Area Chair for ACL and EMNLP and on the program committees of FAccT and AIES.

03 — Reach out

Reasons to say hello.

Thanks for visiting! Here are a few good reasons to get in touch. The list is probably incomplete.

You're a researcher

Questions about a paper, an idea you'd like to brainstorm or debug, or a possible collaboration on agent evaluation, deep research, personas, or responsible NLP.

You're a student

Thinking about a Ph.D. in NLP or HCI, or about doing research in industry? I'm glad to share what I've learned, especially if you're making the jump from engineering like I did.

You're a journalist

Looking for a technical perspective on AI search and deep research, LLM personas, or bias in language technology. My work has appeared in New Scientist, Fast Company, and The Hill.

You're building agents

Shipping an agent or LLM feature and want to think through evaluation, safety, or user simulation before it meets real users.

You're organizing something

A talk, panel, tutorial, or guest lecture on trustworthy agents or sociotechnical NLP.

You're in the Bay Area

Up for coffee and a conversation about NLP, HCI, or responsible AI with like-minded folks? Let's meet.

When in doubt, reach out!
